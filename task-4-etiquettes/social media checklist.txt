The Dos of Responsible Social Media

1. To safeguard your professional reputation, make sure you are only sharing personal updates with the people you intend to share them with by routinely auditing your privacy settings.

2. To create a solid network of contacts that may lead to internships or employment   opportunities after graduation, network with industry professionals and alumni in your field.

3. By posting progress photos or summaries of your work, you can effectively transform your profile into a living portfolio that highlights your academic projects and technical accomplishments.

4. Verify information before sharing it to make sure you aren't disseminating false information, which preserves your reputation as a meticulous learner.

5. Make a good first impression on recruiters and possible partners by curating a professional  bio and profile picture on career-focused platforms.



Social Media Don'ts for Responsibility

1.When you're frustrated, avoid posting impulsive tirades or emotionally charged content because these "heat of the moment" posts can permanently harm your online reputation.

2.To protect your physical and online safety, don't divulge sensitive personal information like your unique dorm room number, student ID, or daily class schedule.

3.A history of negativity or unprofessional behavior is a big red flag for potential employers, so avoid public arguments and cyberbullying.

4.Posting anything that shows unlawful activity or blatant transgressions of your university's code of conduct could result in immediate disciplinary action.

5.A healthy online presence depends on keeping a line between your personal and professional identities, so don't overshare every detail of your personal life.
